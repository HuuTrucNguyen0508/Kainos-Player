Direction 4 of 4Loud

A gig poster, not a dashboard. Chalk white, hard black rules, one magenta that only ever means “on”, and a wide display face that makes song titles the biggest thing on any screen. No shadows, no radius, no blur: the grid is drawn with 3 px lines so structure is visible, and the artwork gets a flat colour slab behind it instead of a glow. Built for people who like their player to feel like printed matter stuck to a wall.

Type pairing

Druk Wide
Display: tab words, titles, headings, scheme names. Uppercase at display sizes is the deliberate voice of this direction; it is not used for small labels, which stay sentence case in Neue Haas Grotesk Text (bold for row data). Rendering here with Impact / Arial Black fallbacks, which are tighter than Druk; expect wider, airier letterforms in the real thing.

Palette

Chalk F7F6F2Black 000000Magenta FF2D7AGrey D8D7D2Album slab
Schemes: Chalk (this), Carbon (black ground, chalk type, magenta), Signal (black on yellow), Blueprint (white on cobalt). Each keeps exactly one “on” colour.

Album light, reinterpreted

The sampled cover colour is pushed to full saturation and poured into a solid slab offset 18 px behind the artwork, like a misregistered screen print. The same colour fills the current row in the library, the source cell in the quality readout, and the 4 px progress bar in the mini player. No blur or alpha anywhere, so it holds up on cheap panels and in sunlight. In Compose: the existing sample, saturation set to 0.9, lightness 0.5, drawn as a plain Box behind the cover.
