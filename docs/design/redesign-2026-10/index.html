Kainos Player: five redesign directions

Static mockups, October 2026. Directions 1–4 came first; direction 5 (Folio) was added after the launcher redesign so the two apps can share one look. Each file shows desktop Home with the Now Playing pane, desktop Library (Songs), and phone Now Playing, Home, Search and Settings → Appearance. Everything shown is an existing feature; nothing is invented. Fonts render with system fallbacks, so the intended faces are named in each header.

1. Liner Noteseditorial

A record sleeve on cool paper: one serif voice for titles, ruled ledgers instead of cards, cobalt for “on”, and album light as ink bleeding through the page.

2. Nocturnecinematic dark

A dark room lit by the record that is playing: the cover blurred across the player, and its sampled colour as the only accent, so the app re-tints for every song.

2, revised. Nocturne v2new palettes, custom seek bar

The same room in three new nights, Darkroom (oxblood), Pine (green-teal) and Smoke (violet-graphite), with an in-page switcher; plus four seek bar studies, with Filament, a lit wire in the cover colour, applied throughout.

3. Faceplateanalog hi-fi

A 1970s integrated amp: aluminium faceplate on a dark chassis, push-buttons with travel, a volume knob, amber readouts, and album light as an LED strip and signal meter.

4. Loudgraphic / brutalist

A gig poster: chalk, 3 px black rules, wide display type for every title, magenta only for “on”, and album light as a flat offset colour slab with no blur anywhere.

5. Foliosibling of the launcher

The music app as another page of the Folio launcher: paper with a dotted grid, ink serif and ghost numerals, hairline ledgers, the launcher's icon tiles and now-playing slip, a ruled seek scale, and the same seven tints in both apps. Album light as an ink wash. An alternative to Nocturne, not a replacement.

6. Caelestiadesktop only, follows the shell theme

The Linux window wears the Caelestia shell's own skin: its rounding scale, Google Sans Flex, 85% surfaces over the wallpaper blur, tonal buttons that square off when on, and the shell's wavy seek bar. Colours are read live from scheme.json; an in-page switcher shows four schemes, one light. Album light stays inside the cover card. The phone keeps Folio; a side-by-side section shows what the two share.

Recommendation: build Nocturne (2)

It is the direction that grows out of what already works in Kainos: the album light you like, the Obsidian-Music-inspired island, the atmospheric Home, and the fact that most of your schemes are dark. It also maps most directly to Compose: a blurred copy of the cover painter behind the Now Playing surface, the existing rememberAlbumLight colour promoted to primary, and glass surfaces via a translucent container colour. Phone Now Playing is the screen you see most, and Nocturne makes it the best of the four.

If you want a sharper break from “another dark music app”, Faceplate (3) is the most characterful and still practical; its contained LED-strip album light is cheaper to render than a blur. Liner Notes (1) is the right choice if you want a light-first app and a library that reads like a catalogue. Loud (4) is the riskiest and the most fun; it would suit a “Signal” scheme inside another direction rather than being the whole app.

Shared conventions across all four: Search is Spotify + YouTube with the autoplay toggle shown off; Library keeps its own fuzzy search, the Local files only and Favorites only chips, Sort and Play all / Play favorites above a sticky header, and six availability states (local, cached, downloading, waiting, unavailable, failed); Now Playing shows the quality readout, heart, shuffle with a clear on-state, repeat off/all/one, seek, volume on desktop, sleep timer and queue, with the back control given its own row away from the artwork; the desktop Home hides its session card while the side pane is open, and the phone Home shows the resume card instead. Nothing shown requires anything web-only.
