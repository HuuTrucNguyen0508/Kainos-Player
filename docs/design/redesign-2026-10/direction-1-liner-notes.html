Direction 1 of 4Liner Notes

The app as a record sleeve: cool uncoated paper, one serif voice for everything the listener cares about (titles, artists, albums), and a quiet grotesk for the machinery around it. Nothing is a card; rows are ledger lines, sections are ruled, the layout breathes like a printed insert. Colour is almost absent so the artwork is the only picture on the page.

Type pairing

GT Sectra Display
for titles, artists and screen headings
Söhne
for controls, labels, metadata and numbers (tabular)
Rendering here with Iowan Old Style / Palatino and Helvetica Neue / Inter as system fallbacks.

Palette

Paper ECEDE8Ink 16181ASlate 5B6168Cobalt 1E3DB8Rule C9CBC5Album wash
Cobalt is the only brand colour, used for “on” states (shuffle, hearts, current track) and local-file identity. Scheme picker offers Paper, Pressing (dark), Vellum (warm light) and Carbon (dark, cobalt accent).

Album light, reinterpreted

The sampled cover colour becomes ink that bled through the paper: a wide, low-alpha wash behind the artwork (radial, blurred, 30% alpha), a 6 px tinted stripe in the content gutter, and the hairline under the quality readout. Same sampling and clamping as today’s AlbumLight.kt; lower saturation (0.35–0.5), lightness 0.62 on paper, 0.45 on Pressing. The mini player carries a 2 px tinted top rule instead of a wash.
