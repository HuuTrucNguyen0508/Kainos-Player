Direction 5 of 5, an alternative to Nocturne

5Folio

The music app as another page of the launcher's almanac. Same paper and dotted grid, same ink serif for the thing that matters on each screen and a ghost numeral behind it, same hairline ledgers instead of cards, and the launcher's own now-playing slip reused as the mini player, so opening Kainos from the home screen feels like turning a page rather than switching apps. The launcher's icon tile, an outlined rounded square with a short coloured underline, becomes the pinned-item tile and the tab dock; the week strip's filled ink circle marks everything that is "on". Dark mode is the launcher's warm charcoal paper, not black, and the tint picker in Settings offers the same seven tints as the launcher, so one choice can run across both.

Type pairing

Iowan Old Style with the system sans
Serif (Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Noto Serif, Georgia fallbacks) for titles, screen names, ghost numerals and tabular time readouts; italic serif for soft accents and section rules. System sans (Roboto on Android, Noto Sans here) at 12–15 px for everything else. Identical stacks to the launcher.

Palette, live from the tint

Accent is the launcher's accent and marks only the active tab, the seek dot and local availability. Spotify and YouTube carry the launcher's green and red hue marks, never logos.

Album light, reinterpreted

An ink wash and a coloured rule
The sampled cover colour (saturation clamped per scheme, as AlbumLight.kt already does) is mixed into the paper at 28% and laid behind the cover as a soft radial wash that fades to paper, and drawn once more as a 44 px rule under the cover, where an app tile carries its mark. The same wash tints the mini slip and the current row in Library. No glow, no blur. In Compose: a radial brush behind the artwork plus one HorizontalDivider coloured by rememberAlbumLight.
