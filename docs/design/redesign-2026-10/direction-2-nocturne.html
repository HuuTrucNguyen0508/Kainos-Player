Direction 2 of 4Nocturne

A darkened room with one light source: the record that is playing. The cover is blurred across the whole player, vignetted into blue-black, and its sampled colour becomes the only accent in the interface, so the app re-tints itself for every song. Type is light, large and close-set; chrome is translucent glass that sits over the glow instead of boxing it in.

Type pairing

Neue Haas Unica, Light and Regular
One family. Light (300/200) at 32–60 px for titles and headings, Regular at 12–15 px for everything else; negative tracking on display sizes only. Rendering here with Helvetica Neue / Segoe UI fallbacks.

Palette

Night 070A12Glass 0E1320Fog E9EDF5Mute 8A93A6Album light
No fixed brand accent. Source dots stay in their brand colours (Spotify green, YouTube red). Schemes vary the base: Night, Ember (warm black), Slate (lighter dark) and one light scheme, Dawn, where the backdrop is a soft tint instead of a blur.

Album light, reinterpreted

Three layers from the same sample: the cover itself scaled 1.5×, blurred 60–70 px and saturated 1.4× behind the whole Now Playing surface; a vertical vignette so text stays readable; and the clamped sample colour (saturation 0.55–0.7, lightness 0.6) used as the live accent for seek fill, active shuffle, hearts, current-row tint and the halo under the artwork. In Compose: Modifier.blur on the cover painter plus the existing rememberAlbumLight colour driving primary.
