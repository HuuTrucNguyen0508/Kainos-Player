Direction 3 of 4Faceplate

A 1970s integrated amplifier, not a streaming app. The player is a brushed-aluminium faceplate screwed onto a dark chassis: push-buttons with travel, a rotary volume knob, an amber readout window for the format and bit depth, and a signal meter that moves with the music. The library is the dark side of the chassis, where the lists live; the player is the bright side you reach for.

Type pairing

DIN Next, Regular and Medium
Faceplate lettering: condensed, even, slightly tracked on the brand line only. Readouts (time, format, counts) use IBM Plex Mono in amber inside dark windows, because the hardware has a display. Rendering here with Bahnschrift / Roboto Condensed / Arial Narrow fallbacks.

Palette

Chassis 2B2825Aluminium D9D3C7Ink 1E1B18Amber F0A030Window 141210Album light
Amber is the only fixed accent: power/on LEDs, hearts, current track. Schemes: Walnut (this), Silver (all-aluminium, light), Black face (dark faceplate, amber), Champagne (warm light).

Album light, reinterpreted

The sampled colour is contained, not diffuse: it backlights the artwork inside the black window (radial, 45% alpha, clipped to the window), lights a 4 px LED strip under the cover, fills the seek bar and the signal meter, and becomes the mini player’s top rule. Same sampling as AlbumLight.kt, saturation clamped 0.5–0.7, lightness 0.55 so it reads as a lit element against aluminium. In Compose the window is a Box with clip and the existing albumLight modifier.
